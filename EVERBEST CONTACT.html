Before you write in

A few things people usually ask first.

How long does a custom pair take?

One to three weeks from your fitting session, depending on leather availability and how deep the queue is that month. Rush orders are sometimes possible — ask when you write in.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, to most countries. Duties and import fees are the buyer's responsibility and vary by destination.

Can I bring in a pair for repair without an appointment?

Drop-ins are welcome during workshop hours. For resoles or structural repairs, we'll usually give you a same-day quote and a pickup window.

Do you take wholesale accounts?

Selectively. Email us with your store, current brands you carry, and expected order volume, and we'll follow up.
